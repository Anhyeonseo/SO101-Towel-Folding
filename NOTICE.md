# Asset attribution

SO-101 geometry and inertial data originate from TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100,
`Simulation/SO101/so101_new_calib.urdf`, revision
`fda892cba81032c46c40976a48c9ceadbf40a9ca`, under Apache-2.0.

The wrist camera and overhead camera mount meshes originate from the same
project's optional camera mounts, under Apache-2.0. This repository includes
metre-scaled variants and a modified overhead mount tip. The dual-arm URDFs
change names, joint conventions, registration and collision geometry. The
2.2 mm surface-matched fixed-jaw pad is a project-generated mesh.

Isaac Sim, Isaac Lab, Newton, Warp and other runtime dependencies are separately
distributed projects under their respective licenses. They are not vendored here.
